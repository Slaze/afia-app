# Afia TV

The Afia TV phone shell and public lineup feed. People can watch Afia TV live, catch up on programmes, and follow news from the station.

## Who it is for

Viewers who want Afia TV on a phone or in a browser, in a compact app-like shell.

## What you can do

- Watch the live channel
- Catch up on programmes
- Follow news and the station lineup
